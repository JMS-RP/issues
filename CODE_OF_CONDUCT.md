# Community Conduct

The JMS-RP issue tracker is a public space for useful, respectful product feedback.

Participants should:

- Be specific and honest.
- Assume good faith while investigating reports.
- Keep criticism focused on the issue, not individuals.
- Avoid harassment, threats, discrimination, spam, and personal information.
- Follow staff instructions when a report involves private or security-sensitive details.

Reports may be edited, labelled, merged, or closed when necessary to keep the tracker useful.
